The Three-Layer Ecosystem

One suite. Roots to canopy
to community.

Like G Suite for productivity, Tree Suite integrates three tools so data flows from citizen capture → city intelligence → human wellbeing.

01 — Capture
🌿
TreeFolio
The Roots

Transforms any smartphone into a professional tree measurement tool using AI (Meta SAM) and AR — no equipment needed. Any citizen can map height, canopy, species, and CO₂ in 5 minutes.

  • AI-powered species ID via PlantNet (20,000+ species)
  • AR distance ruler + Chave 2014 CO₂ equation
  • Community verification consensus system
  • Gamified: Sapling Points, squad mapping missions
  • 5% cost of traditional surveys (₹1 vs ₹20/tree)
Live Demo ↗
02 — Analyze
🗺
Tree Intelligence
The Canopy

City-scale urban forest digital twin. Ward-level heat, CO₂, and green-cover analytics from 1.79M Pune trees + 7 years of satellite data — turning raw census into planning intelligence.

  • Digital twin: every tree CO₂-tagged, geolocated
  • Heat island overlay (LST), 10m green-cover change
  • Ward Green Score: composite equity index
  • Drag-drop planting advisor (5°C cooling prediction)
  • 3D shadow simulation, 60fps for 5k trees
Live Demo ↗
03 — Wellbeing
🧦
Eco-Path Pune
The Community

Gives citizens something back. Generates greener walking routes and GPS-triggered tree meditations — as you pass a mango tree, you hear: “abundance flows like a mango.” Nature as teacher.

  • Routes scored by tree canopy, CO₂, biodiversity
  • Gemini-powered meditation scripts per tree species
  • Offline Piper TTS — no cloud audio cost
  • AQI + water-table overlays (roadmap)
  • Post-walk reflection: journey insights

🌳 Civic Activism
Warrior Moms, Pune

Pune’s grassroots tree-protection group uses Tree Suite data to run RTI campaigns against illegal felling — giving citizens verifiable evidence to challenge construction permits with data, not just protest.
